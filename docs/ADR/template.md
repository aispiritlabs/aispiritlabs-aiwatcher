# ADR_NNNN: <decision, as a sentence>

- **Status**: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR_NNNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision. Constraints, and what was already true.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

Each with the reason it lost. An alternative with no stated reason reads as one
that was never really considered.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it makes harder, and what it rules out. Then:

**What would make this wrong.** The observation that should trigger revisiting
this — a number, a failure mode, a change in the ecosystem. Without it, an ADR
is a decision nobody knows when to reopen.
